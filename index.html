About Us

266 Stockton Squadron is part of the Royal Air Force Air Cadets. We provide young people with opportunities to learn new skills, develop confidence and take part in a wide range of activities.

What We Do

✈️ Aviation

Learn about aviation and aerospace, with opportunities to experience flying.

🧭 Fieldcraft

Develop teamwork, navigation and outdoor skills.

🚀 Space

Explore space, astronomy and the technology behind modern space exploration.

🤖 VEX Robotics

Design, build and programme robots as part of our robotics activities.

🎵 Music

Take part in our developing squadron band and learn musical skills.

🩹 First Aid

Learn practical first-aid skills and work towards recognised qualifications.

Join Us

Interested in becoming a cadet? RAF Air Cadets offers young people the opportunity to take part in exciting activities, develop leadership skills and make new friends.

More information about joining can be found through the official RAF Air Cadets application process.
